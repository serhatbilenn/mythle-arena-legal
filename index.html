Introduction

Mythle Arena ("we", "our", "us") is operated by Onur Şendur. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and disclose it, how long we retain it, and the rights and choices available to you.

By using Mythle Arena, you acknowledge that information will be handled as described in this policy and applicable law.

Scope and Definitions

This policy applies to Mythle Arena, related support channels, and privacy requests handled by Onur Şendur.

Personal data means information that identifies or can reasonably be linked to an individual.

Service providers are vendors that process data on our behalf under contractual or legal obligations.

Third-party services may publish separate privacy notices that also apply when you use those services.

Information We Collect

We collect information that is necessary to provide game functionality, maintain player progress, and operate supported cloud features.

Depending on how you use Mythle Arena, we may collect or process:

  • Identifiers, such as a Firebase User ID
  • Player profile information, such as display name
  • Game progress, levels, XP, achievements, and collection progress
  • Battle history and gameplay statistics
  • Game preferences and settings
  • Cloud save data associated with your account
  • Purchase and entitlement information required to provide purchased features
  • Limited technical or diagnostic information generated by third-party services

Mythle Arena may use anonymous authentication, allowing an account identifier to be created without requiring you to provide an email address or password.

If additional account-linking options such as Sign in with Apple are made available, information provided through those services will depend on the choices you make with the relevant provider.

Payment card details are handled by Apple or the applicable payment processor and are not stored directly by Mythle Arena.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and maintain player profiles
  • Authenticate users
  • Save and restore game progress
  • Synchronize supported game data through cloud services
  • Maintain levels, XP, collections, achievements, and battle statistics
  • Provide purchased or premium features
  • Improve app reliability and functionality
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, or unauthorized use
  • Respond to support and privacy requests
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Sharing / Third Parties

We may share information with service providers involved in operating Mythle Arena, including:

  • Google Firebase
  • Apple

Firebase services may be used for authentication and cloud data storage.

Apple may process information related to App Store purchases, subscriptions, Sign in with Apple, and other platform services.

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable data protection obligations.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to respond to lawful requests, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a reorganization, merger, financing, or sale of assets.

Your information may be processed in countries where our service providers operate. Data protection rules in those countries may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

Firebase Authentication

Mythle Arena may use Firebase Authentication to create and maintain user accounts.

Anonymous authentication may be used to provide account-based functionality without requiring users to manually register.

Firebase Authentication may generate a unique user identifier associated with the user's game data.

If account-linking functionality is provided, an anonymous account may be linked to another supported authentication method.

Cloud Save and Firestore

Mythle Arena may use Cloud Firestore to store and synchronize game information.

Cloud-saved information may include:

  • Player profile information
  • Display name
  • Level and XP
  • Progress
  • Character or card collection
  • Achievements
  • Battle history and statistics
  • Game-related preferences

Cloud Save allows supported information to remain associated with a user account and may allow progress to be restored across compatible sessions or devices.

In-App Purchases

Mythle Arena may offer in-app purchases or subscriptions through Apple's App Store.

Payments are processed by Apple.

We do not directly collect or store full payment card information.

We may receive or process limited transaction and entitlement information necessary to determine whether purchased content or premium features should be available to a user.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy and to meet legal or operational obligations.

Game progress and cloud account information may be retained while the associated account remains active.

Retention periods may vary by data type and purpose.

We may delete, anonymize, or aggregate information when it is no longer reasonably necessary.

Some information may be retained for longer where required for legal compliance, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, accounting, security, or transaction verification.

Account and Data Deletion

Where account-based functionality is available, users may request deletion of applicable account data.

Deleting an account may result in the permanent deletion of associated cloud-saved game progress and profile information.

Some information may be retained where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, transaction records, or legal compliance.

To request access or deletion, contact us at onursendur@gmail.com.
