Privacy Policy

Mythle Arena
Effective date: October 6, 2026

Introduction

Mythle Arena ("we", "our", "us") is operated by Onur Şendur. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected or processed when you use Mythle Arena, how that information is used, how it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

By using Mythle Arena, you acknowledge that information will be handled as described in this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

Scope and Definitions

This Privacy Policy applies to Mythle Arena, its cloud-save functionality, analytics, and support or feedback features.

Personal data means information that identifies an individual or can reasonably be linked to an individual.

Service providers are third parties that process information in connection with operating or supporting Mythle Arena.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Mythle Arena, the following categories of information may be collected or processed:

  • A Firebase user identifier used for authentication and cloud-save functionality
  • Your optional in-game display name
  • Game progress, including XP and Story progression
  • Saved team and deck selections
  • Gameplay statistics such as victories, defeats, and win streak information
  • Onboarding and tutorial completion state
  • App version and build information
  • Device model and operating system version
  • App usage and engagement information processed through Firebase Analytics
  • Feedback, bug reports, or support messages that you choose to submit

Mythle Arena uses anonymous Firebase Authentication. This allows a technical user identifier to be created without requiring you to provide an email address or password.

If Sign in with Apple becomes available and you choose to use it, your anonymous account may be linked to your Apple authentication credential so that supported cloud progress can be restored after reinstalling the app or moving to another compatible device.

Mythle Arena does not request or store your Apple password or raw Apple authentication credentials.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and maintain a player profile
  • Authenticate anonymous or linked users
  • Save and restore supported game progress
  • Synchronize supported game data through cloud services
  • Maintain player XP, Story progression, team and deck selections, and battle statistics
  • Provide onboarding and tutorial functionality
  • Understand general app usage and engagement
  • Improve the stability, usability, and design of Mythle Arena
  • Receive and respond to feedback, bug reports, and support requests
  • Protect the service against abuse or unauthorized access
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Firebase Authentication

Mythle Arena uses Firebase Authentication to create and maintain technical user identities.

Anonymous authentication allows cloud-enabled features to operate without requiring you to create an account with an email address or password.

Firebase Authentication generates a unique Firebase User ID that may be associated with your cloud-saved game information.

Anonymous cloud data alone may not be recoverable after reinstalling the app or moving to another device. If Sign in with Apple is enabled and you choose to link your profile, supported cloud data may be restored using that linked authentication method.

Cloud Save and Cloud Firestore

Mythle Arena uses Google Cloud Firestore to store and synchronize supported game information. Local game data remains the primary gameplay save, and the app is designed to continue working when a network connection is unavailable.

Cloud-saved information may include:

  • Display name
  • Total XP
  • Story completion information
  • Saved team selections
  • Saved deck selections
  • Battle record statistics
  • Onboarding and tutorial completion state
  • App version, build information, and synchronization timestamps

Character and card availability may be derived from Story progression rather than stored as separate cloud records.

Cloud Save is used to back up and synchronize supported progression associated with a Firebase user identity.

Firebase Analytics

Mythle Arena uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and to improve the player experience.

Firebase Analytics may automatically process information such as:

  • App sessions and engagement
  • Device model
  • Operating system and version
  • App version
  • General technical and app-instance information

Mythle Arena may also record limited gameplay events, such as:

  • Onboarding completion
  • Story stage starts and results
  • Player level progression
  • Champion unlock events
  • Successful feedback submission categories
  • Meaningful cloud synchronization events

We do not intentionally send feedback message contents, your display name, Apple authentication credentials, precise location, or other free-form personal content to Firebase Analytics.

Feedback and Support

Mythle Arena provides in-app forms that allow you to send feedback, report a problem, or contact support.

When you submit a message, Cloud Firestore may store:

  • The category of your request
  • The message you entered
  • The date and time of submission
  • App version and build number
  • iOS version
  • Device model
  • Your Firebase User ID
  • Whether the account is anonymous
  • Player level and Story progress

Feedback and support messages are submitted voluntarily. We do not require an email address in order to use the in-app support system.

Sharing and Third-Party Services

We use service providers necessary to operate Mythle Arena, including:

  • Google Firebase
  • Apple

Google Firebase provides services including Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Analytics.

Apple provides operating-system and platform services and may also provide Sign in with Apple if that feature is enabled.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable data protection obligations.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to lawful requests, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a reorganization, merger, financing, or sale of assets.

Information may be processed in countries where our service providers operate. Data protection laws in those countries may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the features described in this Privacy Policy and to meet legal, security, or operational requirements.

Cloud progress and profile information may remain stored while the associated Firebase account exists.

Feedback and support submissions may be retained for the period reasonably necessary to investigate or respond to the request and maintain service records.

Some information may be retained longer where required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other legitimate operational needs.

Account and Cloud Data Deletion

Where cloud account functionality is available, Mythle Arena provides an in-app option to delete the cloud account.

Deleting a cloud account may permanently remove the associated Firebase cloud profile and cloud-saved progress.

Deleting the cloud account does not automatically delete the game progress stored locally on the current device unless a separate local reset function is used.

After deletion, the app may create a new anonymous Firebase identity if cloud functionality is used again.

For privacy-related requests or questions, use the in-app Settings → Support → Contact Support feature.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, applicable privacy law may provide rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Request information about how personal data is used
  • Restrict or object to certain processing where applicable
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

You can submit a privacy request through Settings → Support → Contact Support in Mythle Arena.

We may take reasonable steps to verify a request before processing it and may require information necessary to identify the relevant account or records.

Children's Privacy

Mythle Arena is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If we learn that personal information from a child has been collected in circumstances where parental consent was legally required and was not obtained, we will take reasonable steps to delete the relevant information.

Parents or guardians may submit privacy concerns through the in-app Settings → Support → Contact Support feature.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Cloud Save access is associated with the authenticated Firebase user identity, and Firestore access rules are used to restrict access to supported user data.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Mythle Arena, our service providers, legal requirements, or operational practices.

When appropriate, we may update the effective date or provide notice through the app or this website.
